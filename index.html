About

I got into hardware by fixing my own gear. Car stereos without modern Bluetooth, streams that needed a real camera switcher, apps that ignored media keys.

Most of what I build means wiring breadboards, laying out circuits, soldering and writing firmware in C or Python. Between devices, I write tools that automate my desktop.
